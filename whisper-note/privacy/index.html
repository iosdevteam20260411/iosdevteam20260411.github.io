Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the current version of Whisper Note handles your information when you use the app.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Your recordings stay on your device.

Audio and transcriptions are processed and stored locally. The current version has no advertising, analytics, third-party crash reporting, or account registration.

Audio and transcriptions

Whisper Note processes and stores your audio recordings and transcriptions on your device. This content is not sent to our servers or to an external AI service for processing. We do not have access to your recordings or transcriptions.

Microphone access

Recording audio requires your permission to use the microphone. You can manage or revoke this permission in your device's Settings. Disabling microphone access prevents the app from recording new audio.

Advertising, analytics, and accounts

The current version of Whisper Note does not include advertising, analytics tools, third-party crash reporting integrations, or account registration.

Data sharing

We do not collect your recordings or transcriptions, and we do not sell or share that content with third parties.

Changes to this policy

This policy describes the current version of the app. We will update this page if future features change how information is handled, including the introduction of advertising, analytics, accounts, or online services. The date above indicates the latest revision.
